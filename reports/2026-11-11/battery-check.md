# 18650 battery research — a better cell in the Samsung 35E's class

Checked: Monday, November 2, 2026 (seller pages crawled within the last few days; prices are as found that day and move around).
Job: Clay's current 18650s are not very good; the one that works well is his pink Samsung INR18650-35E. He wants research on a similar-or-better genuine cell. It does not have to be Samsung. No immediate need — a considered buy. No purchase was made.

## The honest frame first

- **No genuine big-maker 18650 holds more than about 3,500 mAh.** Samsung, LG, Panasonic/Sanyo, Sony/Murata, and Molicel all top out there. Any no-name cell printed "4000 mAh" or higher is lying — controlled tests find no 18650 over ~3,650 mAh, and most "4000 mAh" cells test under 3,200 mAh.
- **Printed capacity is the typical number; the guaranteed minimum is lower.** In this class: Samsung 35E — 3,500 printed, 3,350 mAh minimum guaranteed. LG MJ1 — 3,500 printed, 3,400 minimum. Sanyo NCR18650GA — 3,450 typical, 3,350 minimum. In independent tests (HKJ/lygte, Mooch) all three deliver roughly 3,300–3,450 mAh at light-to-moderate drain. They are, in truth, near-identical in capacity.
- **How the class trades:** high capacity means modest discharge. The 35E gives 8 A continuous. The MJ1, GA, and Molicel M35A give 10 A at the *same* capacity — a small free step up in headroom, not a trade. Going further up in discharge (e.g. Molicel P28A, 35 A) costs real capacity (2,800 mAh) — that is a trade, and not the direction Clay asked for.
- **Seller trust is half the answer.** Counterfeit 18650s are rampant, and the counterfeit channel is marketplace listings (Amazon/Walmart third-party sellers, eBay, AliExpress). Neither Amazon nor Walmart sells these bare cells first-party in a verifiable way, so for this buy the known specialist US sellers — 18650BatteryStore, IMR Batteries, Liion Wholesale (plus Battery Junction, Illumn, Orbtronic) — are the trusted road. Every price below is from one of those specialists.

## The field, vetted

### 1. LG INR18650MJ1 — recommended
- Capacity: 3,500 mAh typical, **3,400 mAh minimum — the highest guaranteed floor in the class**. Continuous discharge **10 A** (vs the 35E's 8 A).
- Top: flat top as made by LG. Button-top and protected versions exist, but those are seller-added after LG production (Battery Junction flags its button-top MJ1 as generically re-wrapped — normal for the type, and it adds length).
- Price as checked: **$5.99 per cell, in stock, at IMR Batteries** (specialist road). Also Battery Junction $7.14 flat top, or a 4-pack at $28.08 ($7.02/cell). Liion Wholesale's protected button-top MJ1 (UL 2054 certified) is $9.95 if protection is wanted.
- Warnings: none specific. LG cell, Korean-made, long track record, Mooch-rated at its 10 A datasheet rating. Not discontinued.

### 2. Panasonic/Sanyo NCR18650GA — best value
- Capacity: 3,450 mAh typical, 3,350 mAh minimum. Continuous discharge **10 A** (Panasonic's own sheet suggests 8 A if cycle life matters most). HKJ measured ~3,380 mAh at 1–5 A — right in class.
- Top: flat top (red wrap). Protected button-top versions are seller/pack-maker builds (~$8.85–8.99).
- Price as checked: **$3.94 per cell, in stock, at Liion Wholesale** (specialist road) — the cheapest genuine cell in the class that day. Also 18650BatteryStore $6.99 (10+ at $6.25); IMR $8.99.
- Warnings: same cell is sold as both "Panasonic" and "Sanyo" — both genuine. New stock may print "3300 mAh" on the wrap (a Chinese labeling-rule change to the minimum figure); the cell is unchanged. Not discontinued.

### 3. Samsung INR18650-35E — more of the known-good
- Capacity: 3,500 mAh printed, 3,350 mAh minimum (Samsung's datasheet states no typical figure). Continuous discharge **8 A** — the lowest headroom in this list.
- Top: flat top (pink wrap). Button-top $7.99 and protected button-top $11.99 versions at 18650BatteryStore are seller builds; protected adds 4–6 mm of length.
- Price as checked: **$6.99 per cell, in stock, at 18650BatteryStore** (10+ at $5.50). IMR lists $5.99 but was out of stock.
- Warnings: the 35E is one of the most counterfeited cells there is — the specialist road matters most exactly here. Not discontinued.

### 4. Molicel INR18650-M35A — fine, no edge
- Capacity: 3,500 mAh printed, 3,450 typical / 3,350 minimum per Molicel's datasheet. Continuous discharge **10 A**. Flat top.
- Price as checked: **$6.99 per cell, in stock, at IMR Batteries**; Battery Junction $6.95.
- Warnings: Molicel is a real maker (Taiwan) with a strong name, and Mooch rates this cell well — but it matches the MJ1 on paper and costs a dollar more, so it ranks below it. Not discontinued.

### 5. Vapcell F38 — the only real capacity jump, with a catch
- Capacity: **3,800 mAh typical, 3,650 mAh minimum printed** — above the whole big-maker class. Mooch bench-tested it and was impressed ("wow" in his preliminary post; rated 10 A continuous), and independent charger tests measured ~3,850–3,940 mAh at light drain. On test evidence, the capacity is real.
- Top: flat top; button top can be seller-added.
- Price/availability as checked: Liion Wholesale carried it ("direct from Vapcell") but was **out of stock**; no in-stock price at a trusted US specialist that day.
- Warnings — the catch: Vapcell is a **rewrapper, not a maker**. The cell under the wrap is a Chinese-made cell whose source can change between batches, and Mooch's standing warning is that cells not made by Samsung/Sony/LG/Panasonic/Sanyo "can change at any time," with more cell-to-cell variation. Vapcell also lists an even newer N41 (4,050 mAh claimed, 2025) — too new and unproven to rank. The F38 is the one way to get *more* capacity than the 35E rather than the same, but it gives up the big-maker guarantee to get it.

*Not ranked:* Murata/Sony VTC7 (3,500 mAh, 10 A) exists in pack-builder channels overseas but is not stocked by the trusted US specialists — no clean road to buy it. Panasonic NCR18650G and LG M36 print 3,600 mAh but at only ~5–7 A and are pack-industry cells, not an improvement for general use.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **LG MJ1** — $5.99 at IMR Batteries. Same class capacity as the 35E with the highest guaranteed minimum (3,400 mAh) and 10 A instead of 8 A. The straight step up.
2. **Sanyo/Panasonic NCR18650GA** — $3.94 at Liion Wholesale. Same class, 10 A, cheapest genuine cell found.
3. **Samsung 35E** — $6.99 at 18650BatteryStore. More of exactly what already works for him, if he prefers the known cell.
4. **Molicel M35A** — $6.99 at IMR Batteries. Equal to the MJ1 on paper, a dollar more.
5. **Vapcell F38** — the only true capacity increase (~3,800 mAh), but a rewrap with batch-change risk, and out of stock at the trusted seller when checked.

**Recommendation: the LG MJ1, from IMR Batteries (specialist-seller road), $5.99 per cell as checked November 2, 2026.**

## What still needs Clay's pick

- **How many cells** — prices above are per single cell; the 35E and GA get cheaper at 10+ from 18650BatteryStore.
- **Flat top or button top, and protected or unprotected** — this depends on the device the cells are for, which is not on record here. All prices above are for the plain flat-top unprotected cell as the maker made it. If his device needs a button top or protection, the MJ1's protected button-top version at Liion Wholesale ($9.95, UL-certified) is the vetted option — but protected cells run 4–6 mm longer and do not fit every device.
- Whether the F38's extra capacity is worth the rewrap risk — only if "better" to him means strictly *more* capacity; otherwise the big-maker cells stand.
